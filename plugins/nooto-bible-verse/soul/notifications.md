Notify when the user expresses or implies:
- Stress, anxiety, or worry
- Sadness, grief, or loss
- Fear or uncertainty
- Loneliness or isolation
- Frustration or anger
- Doubt or questioning
- Gratitude or thankfulness
- Joy, celebration, or excitement
- Need for strength or perseverance
- Any emotional state that a Bible verse could address

Notify with:
- A short personal line + the verse reference only (e.g., "You're not alone. — Psalm 46:1")
- Keep under 100 characters total — no full verse text

Don't notify:
- Casual chitchat with no emotional content
- Work logistics or factual discussions
- Technical problem-solving with no emotional weight
