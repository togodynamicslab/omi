Notify when the user expresses or implies:
- Stress, anxiety, or worry
- Sadness, grief, or loss
- Fear or uncertainty
- Loneliness or isolation
- Frustration or anger
- Doubt or questioning faith
- Gratitude or thankfulness
- Joy, celebration, or excitement
- Need for strength or perseverance
- Facing a decision or crossroads
- Dealing with conflict or difficult people
- Temptation or moral struggle
- Seeking purpose or direction
- Financial pressure or provision needs
- Parenting or family challenges
- Work frustrations or career questions
- Health concerns or recovery
- Any situation where Scripture offers guidance or direction

Notify with:
- A short personal line + the verse reference only (e.g., "Trust His plan. — Proverbs 3:5-6")
- Keep under 100 characters total — no full verse text
- Match the TONE to the situation: comfort when hurting, wisdom when deciding, courage when afraid, direction when lost

Don't notify:
- Casual chitchat with no emotional or situational content
- Pure logistics ("meet at 5pm", "pick up groceries")
- Technical problem-solving with no emotional or moral weight
